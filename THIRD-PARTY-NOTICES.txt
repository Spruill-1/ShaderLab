Third-party software in ShaderLab

ShaderLab's programs (ShaderLab.exe, ShaderLabHeadless.exe, ShaderLabTests.exe)
build in the code below through the Support library (ShaderLabSupport.vcxproj).
EffectGraph (https://github.com/Spruill-1/EffectGraph, MIT-0), the engine
ShaderLab links, contains none of it.
